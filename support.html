Support

Gurbani audio player (“the app”) is published by Open Sikh Apps. Use this page to get help with the app, report problems, or suggest improvements.

Contact

Email the sevaadars at contact@opensikhapps.com.

Please include your device model, OS version, and app version when you write. You can also use Give feedback inside the app Settings — it opens an email with version details already filled in.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Playback, bookmarks, history, downloads, and settings stay on your device.
  • How do I listen offline? Open a collection and download tracks (or Download all). Turn on Download on Wi‑Fi only in Settings if you want to avoid cellular data.
  • Can I change the language? Yes — Punjabi and English are available in Settings.
  • What is Simple mode? Larger text and clearer icons, intended to make the app easier to use.
  • Where is the source code? github.com/Open-Sikh-Apps/gurbani-audio-player
